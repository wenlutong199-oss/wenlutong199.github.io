Individual coursework · Fall 2026

Reading business differences in annual reports

Can the language in Intel and NVIDIA’s annual reports distinguish the two businesses? I explored this question through text classification, word representations, and financial context.

PythonText analysisWord2Vec10-K filings

Project details

The question

Do the vocabulary and language of two semiconductor companies contain enough information to identify the company behind a report?

The approach

Organized filings submitted in 2020–2024 with Python and extracted Items 7, 7A, and 8. Used 2020–2023 filings for training and 2024 filings for testing; compared text representations, including Word2Vec.

What I produced

A Jupyter Notebook and written analysis comparing model behavior, visualizations, financial context, and limitations.

How I interpret it

Company classification is a text-analysis task. It does not establish investment value; business performance, risk, and valuation require separate analysis.

Individual coursework · Summer 2026

Turning job postings into structured analysis

A Spark SQL analysis of a Lightcast course dataset: connecting job postings with companies, industries, and locations to examine salary and hiring patterns.

Spark SQLPythonData modelingVisualization
Relational data model
job_postingscompaniesindustrieslocations
4 linked tables4 query outputs
Project details & source

The question

How do salary levels, remote-work postings, and hiring activity vary across occupations, companies, locations, and time in the supplied dataset?

The approach

Created four linked tables and wrote Spark SQL queries with joins, filters, date extraction, aggregation, and median salary calculations. Exported query results to CSV and generated charts.

What I produced

Four analyses: occupation-level salary patterns, top remote-hiring companies in California, monthly California postings, and salary comparisons across selected US cities.

Scope

These results describe the course dataset and the selected filters. They are not a current salary benchmark or a causal estimate of what drives pay.
